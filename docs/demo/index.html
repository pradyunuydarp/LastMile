    A ride’s life

    Four states, one trip

    awaiting_rider→ pending→ in_progress→ completed

    A driver accepts, then the rider approves (pending). The car reaches the station and the rider gets in (in_progress). The car reaches the rider’s destination, the seat frees up (completed). If the rider declines or doesn’t answer in 25 s, the seat goes back and the rider waits again.
